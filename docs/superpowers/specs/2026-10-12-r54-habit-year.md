# R54 — a habit's last 12 months

**Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Status:** the user's request (improvement 7, "moze sve"). · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.81`.

## The change

The habit details window gains "Poslednjih 12 meseci", between Uvid and Podešavanja.

- **`Core.habitYear(habit, logs, today, weekRule)`:**
  - **The grid:** 53 weeks as columns, oldest first, from the week a year back to this week. Each column has 7 days in the week rule's order, so Monday or Sunday comes first as on Navike.
  - **Each day:** `habitDayState` gives the state: done, skipped, missed, open (today), unscheduled (before the start, or a day the habit does not plan) or future. A numeric habit's missed or open day with some value is partial.
  - **`done`:** the number of done days.
  - **`percent`:** `habitCompletionForDates` over the planned days up to today. Skipped days are left out, as in the day share (R8a). It is null when nothing was planned.
  - **`months`:** the columns where a month begins.
- **The window:**
  - **Summary:** "Urađeno dana: N · P% planiranog", or "U poslednjih 12 meseci ništa nije bilo planirano".
  - **Month names:** short names above their columns.
  - **The grid:** one image for screen readers (`role="img"`, named by the summary). Each square carries its date and state as a tooltip for the mouse.
  - **Legend:** Urađeno, Delimično, Preskočeno, Propušteno.
  - **Size:** the squares share the window's width (`minmax(0, 1fr)` columns, square cells), so the year fits without scrolling. That is about 5 px per square on a narrow phone and 11 px in the desktop window.
- **Colors:** tokens only:
  - mint for done;
  - mint at 45% for partial;
  - `--line-3` for skipped;
  - `--graphite-700` for missed and today;
  - empty for days not planned.

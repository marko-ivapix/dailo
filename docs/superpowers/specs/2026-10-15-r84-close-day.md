# R84 — Zatvori dan

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** the user's request from the review list ("sve odradi osim ciljeva"), including the bulk exception ("Da, izuzetak"). · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.111`. No data-model change; one new device-local key.

## The change

- **When:** Danas shows "Zatvori dan" (`closeDayNotice`) from the journal reminder time, or from 20:00 when that reminder is off, while open tasks are planned for today.
  - Past-due tasks have their own section (R83) and are not counted.
  - The card sits before the journal notice and says "Otvoreno danas: N".
- **"Sredi jedan po jedan"** opens a pass (`close-day`) over today's open plan, one task at a time. It reuses the Razvrstaj redom window.
  - **Sutra** plans the task for tomorrow, and **Kad stignem** clears its day. A repeating task asks "Samo ovo / Ovo i buduća".
  - **Datum…** opens the plan picker.
  - **Završi** completes the task.
  - **Otvori** opens the task window, which returns to the pass.
  - **Obriši** asks first, deletes with Undo and comes back to the pass.
  - **Preskoči** moves on.
  - **The end:** "Dan je zatvoren." with the button "Zapiši kako je prošao dan", which opens today's journal entry.
- **"Sve na sutra"** (`moveTodayToTomorrow`, the AGENTS.md exception) moves today's open plan to tomorrow in one step, with "Neurađeno je pomereno na sutra" and Undo.
  - Deadlines stay, and so does the time of day.
  - A repeating task moves only this occurrence (a `recurrenceBaseline` copy).
- **"Ne danas"** hides the card until tomorrow on this device. The device-local key `dailoCloseDayLater` is outside state, backups and sync, and listed in CLAUDE.md.
- **Šta je novo** gets a line, and its range ends at alpha.111.

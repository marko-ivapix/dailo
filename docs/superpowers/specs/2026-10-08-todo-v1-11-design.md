# Dailo V1.11 — Weekly review

**Status:** approved scope (user request 2026-10-08, "Odradi sve tačke"; roadmap Phase 4 "Weekly review").
**Baseline:** V1.10 (Smart Quick Add).
**Roadmap:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-07-release-roadmap.md`, Phase 4.

## Goal

Once a week, one guided page walks through everything that drifts and ends with a recorded "review done":
- empty the Inbox;
- handle overdue and missed plans;
- look at the next 7 days;
- check Goals, Habits and Areas.

## Existing behavior (kept)

- **Today:** the "Daily review" card stays as it is.
- **Overdue:** a task is overdue when its due date has passed (`Core.isOverdue`).
- **Missed plans:** a missed plan has a past planned date and no passed due date (`missed-plan` in `deriveTodaySections`).
- **Row actions:** task rows and their quick actions are the existing ones (`taskRow` with the Inbox and overdue options).
- **No bulk actions:** none are added, per the compatibility rule.

## Design

### A. Pure derivation (`js/core.js`)

`Core.deriveWeeklyReview(state, today, weekStartsOn)` returns:
- `weekStart`: the start of the current week (same rule as Settings → week start);
- `inbox`: active Inbox tasks;
- `overdue`: open tasks with a passed due date, oldest due date first;
- `missedPlans`: open tasks with a past planned date that are not overdue, oldest first;
- `nextDays`: 7 entries for the days after today, `{ date, planned, due }` counts of open tasks;
- `goals`: active goals (status `active` or no status) with `{ goal, health }`, where `health` comes from `Core.getGoalHealth`;
- `habits`: habits with status `active`;
- `areas`: Areas with `{ area, open }`, the count of open tasks whose effective Area it is.

### B. Completion record (persisted)

- **Storage:** `state.settings.weeklyReviews`, an array of `{ weekStart: 'YYYY-MM-DD', completedAt: ISO }`. Newest first, one entry per week, at most 26 entries.
- **`Core.weeklyReviewLog(settings)`** returns the valid, de-duplicated and capped list. Invalid entries are ignored.
- **`Core.recordWeeklyReview(settings, { today, now, weekStartsOn })`** returns the new list, with the current week replaced if it already existed.
- **`Core.weeklyReviewDue(settings, today, weekStartsOn)`** is true on the last three days of the week (week start + 4 … + 6) while the current week has no record.
- **Export and import:** a backup whose `settings.weeklyReviews` is present but not an array of at most 52 valid entries is rejected (`Invalid backup: weeklyReviews`), on export and on import.
- **Unchanged:** no schema or IndexedDB change; a missing field means "never reviewed".

### C. Review page (`js/review-ui.js`, route `#review`)

- **Registration and header:**
  - a new domain module registers `renderRoute` for `{ type: 'review' }`;
  - the header is "Nedeljni pregled", with "Nedelja od {date}" as the subtitle.
- **Six numbered sections:** each has the existing row actions or links. All sections except "Sledećih 7 dana" show a count.
  1. **Isprazni Inbox:** Inbox task rows with the Inbox quick actions.
  2. **Kasni i propušteno:** overdue rows, then missed-plan rows, both with the overdue quick actions.
  3. **Sledećih 7 dana:** one line per day with planned and due counts, plus a link to Upcoming.
  4. **Ciljevi:** active goals with progress and health, each a link to the goal.
  5. **Navike:** active habits with their current streak and completion rate, each a link to the habit.
  6. **Oblasti:** Areas with their open-task count, each a link to the Area.
- **Empty sections:** a section with nothing to show displays a one-line "all clear" text.
- **Finishing:** "Završi nedeljni pregled" (`data-action="complete-weekly-review"`) records the week, shows a toast and renders a done note: "Pregled za ovu nedelju je završen {date}." Up to four previous reviews (this week excluded) are listed under the finish button or the done note.

### D. Entry points

- **Navigation:** the sidebar PROGRESS group and the phone "Još" menu get "Nedeljni pregled" (`ph-clipboard-text`).
- **Today notice:** a quiet notice appears while `Core.weeklyReviewDue` is true. It is styled like the backup reminder and has a "Započni pregled" button. It is never a modal and disappears when the review is recorded.

### E. Out of scope

- A step-by-step wizard with per-step persistence.
- Notes or written reflections per review.
- Statistics charts.
- Notifications when the app is closed.

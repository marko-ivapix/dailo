## Data model

A tasks view's `filters` may hold three new keys. They are kept only when set.

| Key | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `period` | `'today'` or `'thisWeek'` | Planned or due in that range. The week follows Podešavanja → početak nedelje. |
| `maxMinutes` | a positive whole number | Has a duration of at most that many minutes. A task without a duration is left out. |
| `noDue` | `true` | Has no due date. |

- **Why new keys:** they are new keys rather than new values of `dueDate` or `plannedDate`. An older app on another device ignores unknown keys (it shows more results) instead of refusing the data in its backup check.
- **Checks:** backups accept only the values above.
- **Unchanged:**
  - sync carries the filters whole;
  - Schema V3, IndexedDB v1 and ZIP `backupVersion: 2` stay.

## The ready-made views

- Sačuvani prikazi lists "Gotovi prikazi" under the user's views. Each row has an icon, a name, one sentence and "+".
- A tap adds the view as the user's own (`sampleKey: view-ready-v1:<key>`), opens it and shows the toast "Prikaz dodat".
- A view already added, by its key or by its name, is not offered again. If it is deleted, it is offered again.

| View | Filters | Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Do 15 minuta** | `maxMinutes: 15`, open | "Otvoreni zadaci koji traju do 15 minuta (trajanje se zadaje u zadatku)." |
| **Visok prioritet ove nedelje** | `priority: 'high'`, `period: 'thisWeek'`, open | "Visok prioritet, sa planom ili rokom ove nedelje." |
| **Bez roka** | `noDue: true`, open | "Otvoreni zadaci bez roka." |
| **Čekam** | `tagId` of the "Čekam" tag, open | "Zadaci sa oznakom „Čekam“: ono što čekaš od drugih." |

- **The "Čekam" tag:** it is found by name or made once (grey). Quick Add's `#Čekam` then puts a task in the view.
- **The edit window:** "Kad radim ili rok" (Danas / Ove nedelje), "Traje najviše" (15, 30, 60 min) and "Bez roka" (Da) are rows after the exact days. Their sheets work like the others, and "Svejedno" removes the filter. The duration stays a number and "Bez roka" stays `true`.
- **The summary:** it reads, for example, "Traje najviše: 15 min · Bez roka".

# R72 — Još: Planiranje with Projekti, Nedeljni pregled in place of Šabloni

**Date:** 2026-10-14 · **Status:** the user's request: "Kada kliknemo na 'jos' u planiranje treba da bude ciljevi, projekti, redovne obaveze i oblasti. Izbaci šablone a na to mesto stavi nedeljni pregled". · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.99`. No data-model change.

## The change

- **Još → Planiranje:** Ciljevi, Projekti, Redovne obaveze, Oblasti, in that order.
  - The new **Projekti** tile counts the open projects, without archived ones and without the Redovne obaveze groups.
  - It opens Zadaci on the Projekti tab, through the existing `#projects` route.
- **Još → Biblioteka:** Beleške, Dnevnik, Resursi, Oznake, **Nedeljni pregled**, Sačuvani prikazi.
  - Nedeljni pregled takes the place of Šabloni and keeps its line ("Vreme je za nedeljni pregled" or the last date).
- **Šabloni leave the menu.** Templates still work:
  - "Sačuvaj kao šablon" stays in the menus of a task, project, habit and goal;
  - "Iz šablona" stays in the windows;
  - the `#templates` screen still opens by its address.
- **The wide-screen sidebar follows Još:**
  - **Planiranje:** Ciljevi, Redovne obaveze, Oblasti. Projekti already have their own group above.
  - **Biblioteka:** Nedeljni pregled where Šabloni were.

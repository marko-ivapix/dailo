# R73 — Česta pitanja (FAQ)

**Date:** 2026-10-14 · **Status:** the user's request: "Mislim da bi trebalo i da napravimo faq". R57 had left a FAQ for a later landing page; the user now asks for one. · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.100`. No data-model change.

## The change

- **`pitanja.html`:** a public Serbian page in the style of `uslovi.html`, with 23 questions in five groups (Osnovno, Podaci i nalog, Korišćenje, Podsetnici, Pomoć).
  - The questions fold open with `<details>`. There is no script; the page has its own CSS, like the other public pages.
  - The answers name what the app shows now:
    - **Account and data:** no account needed; data on the device and, with an account, in the EU (Frankfurt); moving data by account or by Izvezi ZIP / Uvezi ZIP; restore with `VRATI`; "Zaboravljena lozinka?"; the encrypted journal; signing out; deleting the account with `OBRIŠI`.
    - **Using the app:** the "+" per screen, Inbox / Danas / Zadaci ("Kad stignem"), the swipe and hold gestures, the three habit states, projects and areas, Redovne obaveze, Nedeljni pregled in Još → Biblioteka.
    - **Reminders:** in the browser only while Dailo is open; in the phone app also while it is closed.
    - **Help:** reporting a problem, with the `REPORT_EMAIL` address.
  - It has the meta description and the Open Graph set from R57, the "Otvori Dailo" link and no gendered forms.
- **The app:** Podešavanja → Pomoć gets "Česta pitanja · Otvori pitanja" under Uputstvo (`data-faq`). `Release.FAQ_URL` is the online address, and the phone app opens the page online, like the guide and the legal pages.
- **The site:** `tools/check-links.mjs` checks the page, and `404.html` links it.
- **Not in the offline shell:** like `uputstvo.html`, the page is not precached.

## Known discrepancy (not changed here)

`uputstvo.html` still describes the beta without an account and without sync, the restore word `RESTORE` and "Podešavanja → O aplikaciji". The source now has accounts and sync, the word `VRATI` and "Podešavanja → Pomoć". The FAQ follows the source. Updating the guide is a separate step, if the user wants it.

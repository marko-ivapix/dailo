# R91 — Kalendar: 3 dana, the deadline mark, dragging on a phone

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** the user's request from the review list ("sve odradi osim ciljeva"). · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.118`. No data-model change; `ui.calendarView` also accepts `three`.

## The change

- **"3 dana":** a view after Nedelja (`VIEWS = week, three, month, upcoming`).
  - Three days stand side by side as columns, also on a phone. Each column has its day's cards (timed tasks, goal deadlines, the rest), the coming repeats (dotted, with the repeat icon) and "+ Dodaj".
  - The columns start on today or a multiple of three days from it (`threeDayStart`), so picking a day keeps the columns.
  - The arrows move three days ("Prethodna 3 dana" / "Sledeća 3 dana").
  - The day's panel (Lista / Raspored) stays under the columns.
- **The deadline mark.** A task that is in the calendar only for its deadline (no planned day) carries the hourglass, the app's sign for "Rok":
  - **Cards:** an amber dashed border, the icon, and "Rok:" for screen readers.
  - **The day list, Predstojeće and Raspored's "Rok ovog dana":** an amber bar at the row's edge and the icon before the title (`calendarTaskRow(task, date)` → `dueItem`).
  - **Month titles on a wide screen:** an amber edge.
- **Dragging on a touch screen** (`handleCalendarTouchStart` / `Move` / `finish`):
  - **What can be held:** a card, a row of the day's list, a Raspored block or a task without a time. Hold it for 0.4 s, then drag it.
  - **Where it can land:** a day of the week strip, a 3-day column, a month day (which now has `data-calendar-date`, so a mouse drop works there too) or an hour of Raspored. The target is outlined.
  - **Release:** `dropTaskOnDay`, the same as a mouse drop, with Poništi and the repeat question; the click after the drag is swallowed.
  - A swipe on the row gives way once the task is lifted. The page does not scroll during the drag, and a long press opens no menu.
- **Pages:** the guide gets a paragraph. **Šta je novo** gets a line, and its range ends at alpha.118.

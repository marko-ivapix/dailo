# R99 — Šabloni: ready-made templates, and the repeat apart from "made once on a day" (data model)

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** the user's request from the review list ("sve odradi osim ciljeva"). For Šabloni the plan read: "Ugrađena galerija (put/pakovanje, selidba, ispit, jutarnja rutina); zakazano kreiranje sakriveno ili jasno odvojeno od ponavljanja." · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.127`.

**Data model:** a habit template's data may hold `routine` (`'morning' | 'daily' | 'night'`).
- **Why:** "Sačuvaj kao šablon" dropped a habit's routine, and the gallery's Jutarnja rutina needs it.
- **Old data:** a template without the field makes a habit with the usual `'daily'`, as before.
- **Checks:** backups refuse any other value.
- **Unchanged:**
  - sync carries template data whole;
  - Schema V3, IndexedDB v1 and ZIP `backupVersion: 2` stay.

## Where the gallery lives

- Since R72 Šabloni is not in Još or the sidebar. Templates are met in **Iz šablona**, in the new project, habit and goal windows, and in Brzo dodavanje when the user has task templates.
- So the gallery goes where a template is chosen:
  - **Iz šablona** in the new project window offers Pakovanje za put, Selidba and Ispit;
  - in the new habit window it offers Jutarnja rutina.
  - They show under "Gotovi šabloni", after the user's own templates of that type.
  - The chip now shows when the user has templates of that type or the gallery has one (R20 showed it only for the first).
- **Brzo dodavanje is unchanged.** Its "Iz šablona" chip still shows only with the user's own task templates, so capture stays quick. The gallery has no task template.
- **Šabloni (`#templates`)** lists the gallery under the user's templates, with one sentence. A row opens a sheet with:
  - **Upotrebi šablon**: the new-item window, filled;
  - **Dodaj u moje šablone**: the user's own copy, to change; toast "Šablon dodat: …".
- An entry already among the user's templates, by its `sampleKey` (`template-gallery-v1:<key>`) or by name, leaves the gallery. It comes back if that template is deleted.

## The gallery

All four are built with `Core.templateFromEntity` (`js/starter-examples.js`: `galleryEntries`, `galleryTemplate`), so they pass the same checks as the user's own. Picking one in a window fills the draft and saves nothing (`chooseTemplate(null, template)`); the project or habit is saved as usual.

- **Pakovanje za put** (project, five tasks with checklists):
  - Dokumenti: lična karta ili pasoš, zdravstvena kartica, karte i rezervacije, gotovina i kartice;
  - Odeća: majice, pantalone, donji veš i čarape, jakna, udobna obuća;
  - Higijena: četkica i pasta za zube, šampon, dezodorans, lekovi;
  - Elektronika: punjač za telefon, power bank, slušalice, adapter za utičnicu;
  - Pre polaska: zatvori prozore, isključi uređaje iz struje, iznesi smeće, zaključaj vrata.
- **Selidba** (project, seven tasks):
  - dogovori kombi ili selidbenu firmu;
  - nabavi kutije i selotejp;
  - spakuj sobu po sobu (kuhinja, dnevna soba, spavaća soba, kupatilo);
  - prijavi novu adresu (policija (lična karta), banka, poslodavac);
  - prebaci račune (struja, internet, voda, grejanje);
  - očisti stari stan;
  - očitaj brojila i predaj ključeve.
- **Ispit** (project, eight tasks):
  - prijavi ispit;
  - saznaj šta ulazi u ispit;
  - nabavi knjige i skripte;
  - napravi plan učenja po danima;
  - prođi gradivo prvi put;
  - uradi stare rokove;
  - ponovi gradivo;
  - spremi stvari za dan ispita (indeks ili studentska karta, lična karta, olovke, voda).
- **Jutarnja rutina** (habit): a daily checkbox habit in the morning routine, with a 07:00 reminder.
- No dates are set: the trip, the move and the exam each have their own day, which the user sets.
- No goals (the user: "osim ciljeva").

## The repeat apart from "made once on a day"

- In the task template editor (under "Više opcija") the repeat is its own part, **Ponavljanje**; its first field is now "Koliko često".
- Making the task automatically on a date is another part, **Napravi jednom, na zadati dan**, with the sentence "Tog dana Dailo sam napravi jedan zadatak iz ovog šablona. To nije ponavljanje: za zadatak koji se vraća koristi Ponavljanje." Before, it sat right after the repeat fields as one more option.
- The Šabloni list adds what a task template does: "Ponavlja se", or "Pravi se jednom, <datum>" until that day has come.

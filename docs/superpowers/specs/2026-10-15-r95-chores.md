# R95 — Redovne obaveze: a bar instead of a strict deadline, one sentence, seasonal examples

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** the user's request from the review list ("sve odradi osim ciljeva"; for Redovne obaveze: a bar "koliko je vreme" instead of a strict deadline, one sentence on how a chore differs from a repeating task, more seasonal examples). The review behind it: "Umesto strogog roka prikažite traku „koliko je vreme" koja se polako puni (kao Tody). To stvara manje krivice i jasno se razlikuje od zadatka." Household sharing waits for shared accounts. · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.122`. No data-model change: a new chore simply leaves the existing `dueDate` empty.

## The change

- **The bar.**
  - `Core.recurrenceSpanDays(rule)` is about how many days lie between two times of a repeat: daily N, weekly 7·N (split by the chosen weekdays), monthly 30·N, yearly 365·N.
  - `Core.choreProgress(task, rule, today)` returns `{ span, left, fill, state }`, read from the deadline first and then the planned day, as on the screen:
    - `fill` is 0 right after the last time and 1 on its day;
    - `state` is `calm`, then `due` from its day, and `late` once half an interval (at least a day) has passed;
    - it is null without a date or a repeat.
  - Each open row on Redovne obaveze, in "Ove nedelje" and in the groups, shows it under the meta line (`taskRow` option `bar` → `chore-bar`): blue, amber when due, red when late.
- **No strict deadline on this screen.**
  - A row whose day has passed reads "Vreme je · <datum>" in amber (`is-due-now`), not a red "Kasni".
  - The header counts what is due now ("· 3 na redu"); the old "kasni" count is gone.
- **New chores get only a planned day.** This covers the "Nova redovna obaveza" window and the apartment and house examples.
  - A missed chore is then suggested again on Danas ("Predlozi za danas") instead of turning red under Zakasnelo.
  - A deadline can still be set in the task window when there really is one.
  - Existing chores and the "Popuni primerima" groups (registration, bills; R71) keep their dates.
- **One sentence** under the header, and in the empty state: "Za razliku od ponavljajućeg zadatka, obaveza nema strog rok: traka se polako puni dok ne dođe vreme, a kad je uradiš, kreće ispočetka."
- **Seasonal examples.**
  - A chore's first day can be a day of the year ('MM-DD', or a list for one that comes twice a year). It starts on the next such day after today, so the examples never pile up on the day they are added.
  - **Both examples:**
    - Zameni zimsku i letnju garderobu (every 6 months: 1 April, 1 October);
    - Servisiraj klimu pre leta (1 May);
    - Očisti terasu za leto (15 April).
  - **The house adds, in Bašta:**
    - Pregledaj krov posle zime (20 March);
    - Otvori spoljnu česmu (1 April);
    - Pripremi baštu za zimu (25 October);
    - Zatvori spoljnu česmu pre mraza (1 November);
    - Očisti oluke (5 November).
  - As the user asked in R68, there is no smoke alarm and no boiler service. Car chores are already in "Popuni primerima" (R71).
- **Pages:** the guide gets a "Redovne obaveze" section. **Šta je novo** gets a line, and its range ends at alpha.122.

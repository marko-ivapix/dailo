# R62 — windows that keep still, task rows that swipe after a hold

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Status:** the user's report: "kada držim task, ne mogu da ga pomeram levo i desno (jer je pisalo da ima neka funkcija), selektor tekst ispod. Modali kada se promeni nesto znaju da se zatvore i otvore i bude kao seckanje. Mislim da se to moze proveriti kada se dodaje redovni obaveza i klikne se npr na kuhinju ili dnevnu sobu". · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.89`. No data-model change.

## The causes

- **The flicker.**
  - `renderModal` redraws `#modal-root` from scratch on every change, for example a tap on the Kuhinja chip in "Nova redovna obaveza".
  - `.modal-backdrop` (`fade-in`) and `.modal` (`modal-in`) carry entrance animations, so each redraw played the entrance again. The window looked as if it closed and opened.
  - The window's scroll also went back to the top.
  - Sheets (`refreshSheet`, `setPopoverContent`) already redraw inside the same element, so they do not replay their animation.
- **The swipe.**
  - The swipe set `touch-action: pan-y` on the row in its `pointerdown` handler. Browsers decide at the touch start who handles a sideways move, so on a phone the browser took it and ended the swipe with `pointercancel`.
  - After a hold of 350 ms on Danas, Inbox or a project, the row lifted for reordering, which dropped the swipe. A sideways move after the hold did nothing.
- **The selected text.**
  - Only draggable rows had `user-select: none`.
  - The lifted row has `pointer-events: none`, so the long press (about 500 ms) reached the text under it and selected it ("selektor tekst ispod").

## The change

- **A window redrawn after a change keeps still.**
  - `renderModal` marks the new backdrop `is-steady` when the window type is the same as before the redraw.
  - It keeps the scroll of the backdrop and of the window.
  - CSS: `.modal-backdrop.is-steady, .modal-backdrop.is-steady .modal { animation: none; }`, which also wins over the wide-screen `.modal-backdrop-quick .modal` animation.
  - A newly opened window (another type, or the same type after closing) still animates in.
  - A window counts as on screen from the frame after it opened (`modalOnScreen`, set in `requestAnimationFrame` with a token). A redraw before that frame still animates in, because the first drawing was never shown.
- **Task rows on a touch screen** (`@media (pointer: coarse)`):
  - **`.task-row`:** `touch-action: pan-y` from the start, then `pan-y pinch-zoom` where that is understood, so pinch zoom still works there. It also gets `user-select: none` and `-webkit-touch-callout: none`.
  - **While a row or a subtask is lifted** (`.task-drag-active` on the page), nothing selects text. Subtask rows get no rule of their own, because editing a subtask puts a text field inside its row.
  - This replaces the narrower R25 rule for draggable rows.
- **Hold, then move to the side.**
  - The lifted row keeps the waiting swipe (`reorder.swipe`).
  - Until it has moved more than 12 px up or down, a move of more than 12 px that is more sideways than vertical puts it down and continues as the swipe. Right completes the task; left opens the date sheet.
  - Once it has moved up or down, it stays a reorder.
- **No scroll under a swipe:** `blockTouchScroll` also cancels the page scroll while a row is swiped sideways, unless the scroll already started (`cancelable: false`).
- **No long-press menu:** the `contextmenu` guard also covers a row being swiped.
- **The hint on Danas** now says the hold reorders up or down: "Prevuci zadatak udesno da ga završiš, ulevo da mu izabereš datum. Drži red pa ga prevuci gore ili dole da promeniš redosled."

# R50 — sync in real time (Supabase Realtime)

**Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Status:** the user's request (improvement 2, "moze sve"). · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.77`.

## Before

A device synced:
- after its own changes;
- when it came back to the front or online;
- every five minutes.

So a change from another device could take up to five minutes to show.

## The change

- **The client:** `DailoSync.createRealtime({ url, key, onChange, onStatus })` in `js/sync.js`. It has no dependencies and speaks the Phoenix channel protocol 1.0.0 over `wss://<project>.supabase.co/realtime/v1/websocket?apikey=…&vsn=1.0.0`.
  - **Joining:** it joins `realtime:dailo-records` for `postgres_changes` on `public.records`, filtered by `user_id=eq.<account>`, with the user's access token. Row Level Security applies to the channel as well.
  - **Messages:** a `postgres_changes` message only calls `onChange`. The app then runs its usual sync after 800 ms, so the pull, the shadow and every safety rule stay exactly as they were. A missed message costs only time; the five-minute round stays as a fallback.
  - **Keeping the socket alive:** a heartbeat goes out every 25 s.
  - **Reconnecting:** a closed socket or a refused join comes back after 1, 2, 5, 10 and then 30 s; a successful join resets the pause. A refreshed access token is sent on the open channel (`access_token`) instead of reconnecting.
  - **`disconnect()`:** stops everything and leaves no timer behind.
  - **`status()`:** `off`, `connecting` or `live`.
- **The app:**
  - **Opening:** `connectLiveSync()` opens the socket after a successful sync while the app is in front.
  - **Closing:** going to the background closes it, and coming back runs a sync, which opens it again. Sign-out, account deletion and an expired session (401) close it too.
  - **Nalog:** next to "Poslednja sinhronizacija" it says "promene stižu odmah" while the socket is live.
- **CSP:** `connect-src` adds `wss://*.supabase.co`.
- **Server:** `supabase/migrations/0004_realtime.sql` adds `public.records` to the `supabase_realtime` publication, once. Without it the channel joins but hears nothing, and sync works as before. The Supabase guide gains step 6.
- **Echoes:** a device also hears its own pushes, and the extra pull that follows finds nothing new. That is accepted, since filtering echoes could drop another device's change that arrives at the same moment.

## Checks

- **Node:** `tests/realtime-r50.test.js`, written first; it failed 9/9. It runs a fake socket and fake timers and covers:
  - the URL and the join;
  - pokes and ignored messages;
  - the heartbeat;
  - the growing pauses and the reset;
  - `disconnect`;
  - a refused join;
  - a token refresh;
  - no socket and no session;
  - the app wiring;
  - Nalog, the migration and the guide;
  - the version.
- **Older harnesses:** the harnesses that run app.js's sync block in `vm` get `liveSync: null`.
- **Manual-pending:** two real devices with migration `0004` run, and the socket in the Capacitor WebView.

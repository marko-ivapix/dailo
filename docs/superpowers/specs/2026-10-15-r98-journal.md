# R98 — Dnevnik: questions about the day, what was completed, mood and habits

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** the user's request from the review list ("sve odradi osim ciljeva"; for Dnevnik: "pitanja za dnevnik ne treba da budu zadatak, vec pitanja o tom danu"). The review behind it: an empty entry gives no start, and the journal says nothing back about mood. · **Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.125`. No data-model change: the question is put into the entry's existing text, and the insight reads the existing journal moods and habit logs.

## The change

- **A question about the day:** the entry window shows one question about the day under the mood (`Core.journalQuestion(date, offset)`).
  - Ten short questions, the same one all day, the next day the next one ("Šta je danas bilo dobro?", "Za šta danas osećaš zahvalnost?", "Kako se osećaš na kraju dana?" …).
  - The questions are about the day, never a task; nothing is created from them.
  - **Drugo pitanje** shows the next one (`modalState.questionOffset`, the window only).
  - **Upiši** puts the question on its own line at the end of the text and leaves the cursor after it; the usual autosave keeps it.
  - The Serbian wording avoids gendered verb forms.
- **Completed that day:** under the day's summary the entry lists the tasks completed that day, in the order they were done (`Core.completedOn`), under "Završeno tog dana". The list is read-only.
- **Mood and habits:** the Journal screen (the current month, no search) shows a card "Raspoloženje i navike" (`Core.moodInsights`).
  - For each active habit it compares the average mood (1–5) over the last 90 days on the days with the habit done against the other days with a mood.
  - A line shows only when each side has at least three days and the averages differ by 0.3 or more: "Dani sa „Šetnja“: 4,2 · ostali dani: 3,0".
  - At most two lines, the largest differences first. Without them there is no card.
- **The lock, checked:** in the phone app the journal lock (⋯ on the Journal screen) already opens with a fingerprint, the face or the phone's PIN (`@aparajita/capacitor-biometric-auth`, `allowDeviceCredential: true`). The iPhone's Face ID text is in `Info.plist`. On the web the option does not show. Nothing changes here; the guide says so.
- **Pages:**
  - the guide gets a "Dnevnik" section after "Nedeljni pregled": the question, the insight and the lock;
  - **Šta je novo** gets a line, and its range ends at alpha.125.
- **Cleanup:** R97 had added a second "Notes and resources" entry to `js/i18n-sr.js`. The duplicate is removed; the original stays.
